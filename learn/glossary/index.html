10-Percent Doubling Rule

10-Percent Doubling Rule is a race-cube shortcut: the player on roll can generally double when their adjusted count is within about 10 percent of the other player’s count. Knowing this term makes it easier to follow backgammon lessons and analysis.

8-9-12 Doubling Rule

Players use 8-9-12 Doubling Rule to describe a race-cube guideline: double at about 8 percent behind, redouble at 9 percent, and take up to roughly 12 percent behind. Knowing this term makes it easier to follow backgammon lessons and analysis.
