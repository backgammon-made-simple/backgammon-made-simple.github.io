# Quick human UX check

Against a current preview, inspect desktop `1440 x 1000` and mobile
`390 x 844`:

1. Open Home and each primary navbar destination once; ordinary pages must not
   inherit Learn or Research tools.
2. Search and clear `/learn/`, open the Cube track, and inspect title wrapping.
3. On the cube lesson, scroll both directions, use the rails, term lookup, one
   cube answer path, and a nested disclosure.
4. On the checker lesson, select all three candidates; board and metrics must
   change together.
5. Exercise one Research article and the Glossary search/lookup.
6. At mobile width, repeat the lesson, checker, Research, and Glossary actions.
7. Record console errors, failed requests, overlap, clipping, and page-level
   horizontal overflow.

Anything not exercised is `NOT RUN`.
